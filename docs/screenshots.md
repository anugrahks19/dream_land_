# Dreamback — Screenshot Capture Guide

Launch `http://localhost:8000/?debug=1` and press `P` to toggle Photo Mode (hides HUD).

1. **Tail Marshes (Zone 1)** — Press `1` (spawns near colony at `-255, 0`). Angle camera looking northeast across the cottages, stone tower, lily pool, and grazing Puffs during **exhale** (`breath ≈ 0.25`) for warm clear golden light.
2. **Spine Forest (Zone 2)** — Press `2` (`-108, 0`). Frame Bramble, tall golden Honeytrees, and glowing green exhale vines (`breath ≈ 0.4`, exhale phase).
3. **Rib Caverns (Zone 3)** — Press `3` (`52, 0`). Press `H` to wake the Wisp-Moths, then capture the colossal pearl rib arches, teal/pink mushroom pads, and glowing rune gate (`breath ≈ 0.8`, high rib heave).
4. **The Crown & Ending Bloom (Zone 4)** — Press `4` (`224, 0`), then press `K` to trigger the Ending sequence. Capture at **~8 seconds into the ending** during the high pullback as the 3,600-flower wave sweeps across all four zones under the pink-gold dawn sky.
