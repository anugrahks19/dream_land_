# Dreamback — 60-Second Gameplay Video Script

- **0:00–0:10 (Hook — Title & Marshes)**
  - Shot: Title drift over golden-hour Marshes cottages & stone lookout tower → Pip walking out of the colony.
  - Narration: *"What if the open world was alive? Meet Dreamback—a cozy adventure on the back of a sleeping Giant."*
- **0:10–0:22 (The Living Breath)**
  - Shot: Pip pauses on a mossy hill; terrain visibly heaves, fog thickens then clears, reeds sway, breath ring fills.
  - Narration: *"One global Breathing Cycle moves the entire landscape, shifts the fog, drives the wind, and times every puzzle."*
- **0:22–0:34 (Spine Forest — Vines & Wind)**
  - Shot: Pip leaps onto a glowing exhale vine, swings across the amber Honeytree canopy, grabs Fragment 2.
  - Narration: *"Ride exhale gusts and breath-synced vines across the Spine Forest…"*
- **0:34–0:46 (Rib Caverns — Hum & Wisp-Moths)**
  - Shot: Pip holds `H` to send a golden hum ring; Wisp-Moths awaken under giant pearl rib arches, lighting the 3 mushroom pads and sliding open the rune gate.
  - Narration: *"…hum to awaken Wisp-Moths beneath cathedral rib arches…"*
- **0:46–0:56 (The Crown & Ending Bloom)**
  - Shot: Pip weaves past pink-gold Dream Surge orbs to the Dream Altar; camera pulls high across the 600-unit back as 3,600 flowers bloom in a dawn wave.
  - Narration: *"…and return all four dreams at The Crown so Old Mother Hush sleeps peacefully on."*
- **0:56–1:00 (Close)**
  - Shot: Final dawn card *"The Giant sleeps on. The colony is safe."*
  - Narration: *"Built with no game engine, pure JavaScript and Three.js. Thank you!"*
