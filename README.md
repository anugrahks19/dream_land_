# 🌿 DREAMBACK

**A tiny ant. A sleeping giant. An entire world that breathes.**

What if the open world you explored was alive?

Dreamback is a whimsical 3D open-world exploration game where you play as **Pip**, a tiny ant travelling across the enormous back of a sleeping giant.

But this is no ordinary landscape.

The ground beneath your feet rises and falls. Forests sway with every exhale. Fog rolls across distant valleys. Paths shift with the giant's breathing, and the environment itself responds to the way you explore it.

Every hill, cave, forest and hidden corner belongs to something much larger than you.

And somewhere along this living landscape lies a mystery waiting to be uncovered.

---

## 📖 THE STORY — A WORLD ASLEEP

Somewhere beyond the familiar world, there is a giant who has been sleeping for longer than anyone in the ant colony can remember.

Its enormous body stretches across an entire landscape. What appears to be a mountain range is actually its back. What looks like a forest grows across its spine. The great arches of a mysterious cavern are its ribs. And beyond the highest summit lies its head.

For the tiny creatures living here, this is not a giant.

**This is their entire world.**

Pip is a little ant from a peaceful colony living near the giant's tail. Life has always followed a familiar rhythm: the ground gently rises, the wind changes direction, and the landscape moves as though it has a heartbeat of its own.

The colony has learned to live with these strange natural rhythms.

But something is changing.

The giant's breathing is becoming increasingly restless. The ground trembles unexpectedly. Familiar paths begin to shift. The wind carries Pip into unfamiliar places, and areas that once felt safe become difficult to navigate.

Nobody knows why.

The older creatures speak of forgotten places, strange lights and fragments connected to the giant's dreams. Some believe these fragments hold a connection to the giant's sleeping state. Others believe they are simply remnants of a world that existed long before the colony.

Pip sets out into the vast landscape.

There is no single road to follow. No fixed route through the wilderness. Beyond the familiar colony, the world opens in every direction, inviting Pip to wander through marshes, forests, hidden caverns and distant highlands.

Along the way, Pip encounters creatures who have made their homes in unexpected places, discovers forgotten corners of the giant's back, and gradually uncovers the relationship between the land, its breathing rhythm and the mysterious Dream Fragments.

The deeper Pip explores, the more the world begins to make sense.

Perhaps the giant is not simply sleeping.

Perhaps the entire world is dreaming.

And Pip may be the only one small enough to discover what that dream contains.

The journey is not about conquering the giant. It is about understanding the world that exists upon it — and finding a way to let it sleep peacefully.

## 🌍 EXPLORE A LIVING OPEN WORLD

Dreamback is designed around exploration and environmental discovery.

The giant's back is one continuous `780 × 340` landscape rather than a collection of disconnected levels. Each region has its own atmosphere, terrain, discoveries and environmental behaviour.

You are free to wander, revisit places, discover hidden corners and decide how you approach the world.

There is a larger story connecting the landscape, but exploration is not restricted to following a mission list — every quest and fragment is completely optional.

### The Four Regions

#### 1. Tail Marshes — Where the Journey Begins
The quiet beginning of the giant's enormous landscape.

*Green marshlands, shallow pools, moss-covered rocks, drifting fireflies and tiny ant settlements.*

The Tail Marshes introduce Pip to the strange relationship between the environment and the giant's breathing. Reed-covered paths conceal small discoveries, while calm areas offer places to recover and observe the surroundings.

**Grandma Moss-Ant** lives here. She knows more about the giant's world than she initially reveals and shares wisdom about the Hush Lantern.

#### 2. Spine Forest — A Forest That Moves
*Golden trees, hanging vines, drifting leaves and pathways shaped by the wind.*

The forest is constantly affected by the giant's breathing. Branches sway, vines move with the wind, and powerful gusts travel through certain parts of the landscape. What appears to be an unreachable area may become accessible when the conditions change.

**Bramble the Beetle** is one of the creatures Pip may encounter here.

#### 3. Rib Caverns — Beneath the Surface
*Enormous rib-like structures, glowing mushrooms, hidden passages and mysterious underground life.*

The deeper regions of the giant's back reveal a completely different environment. The cavern floor responds strongly to the breathing cycle. Certain platforms appear and disappear depending on the giant's inhalation and exhalation.

**Wisp-Moths** inhabit these caves. Their glowing movements reveal patterns and help Pip understand the environment.

#### 4. The Crown — Beyond the Summit
*Pale stone landscapes, drifting clouds, distant horizons and the enormous silhouette of the sleeping giant's head.*

The highest region of the world offers a different perspective on everything Pip has explored. Here, the scale of the giant becomes impossible to ignore. The **Dream Altar** lies within this region, connecting the fragments discovered throughout the landscape.

At the summit, Pip approaches the final mystery of the giant's dream.

## ⚠️ FIVE ENVIRONMENTAL CHALLENGES OF THE LIVING WORLD

Exploring the giant's back is not simply about moving from one location to another. The environment is alive, unpredictable and constantly changing.

### 01 — The Breathing Landscape
The world itself is constantly moving. The giant's breathing controls a global environmental cycle:
- During inhalation, the terrain rises and the atmosphere shifts.
- During exhalation, the landscape settles and stronger winds travel across the world.
- Certain platforms and pathways respond to the breathing phase.
- Different regions react with different intensities.

### 02 — The Restless Giant
Every disturbance has consequences. Falls, wrong puzzle steps, sprinting near sleeping creatures, and unguarded world events increase the giant's restlessness. Calm Spots and the Hush Lantern offer places and tools to recover and keep exploring.

### 03 — The Shifting Terrain
A path that exists now may not remain accessible forever. Breath-synced platforms appear and fade with inhalation and exhalation, wind corridors propel Pip across gaps, and vines swing in sync with the giant's exhale.

### 04 — Hidden Paths and Environmental Puzzles
Not everything reveals itself immediately. Reed clearings, glowing Wisp-Moth resonance trails, and hidden **Dreamfire Echoes** only reveal themselves when Pip observes, experiments, and pulses the **Hush Lantern (`H`)**.

### 05 — The Dream Surge
At the Crown, lifting the final Dream Fragment triggers the **Dream Surge** — swirling dream orbs drift across the summit alongside powerful gusts as Pip navigates to the Dream Altar.

## ⚡ FIVE DYNAMIC LIVING-GIANT SURVIVAL EVENTS

Because you are roaming across a living, sleeping giant, **five sudden open-world survival events** can strike anywhere on the map at any time:

| # | Event | What Happens | How to Survive |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **🌋 Giant Tremor** | Ground shockwave rings, leaping rock debris, and violent quakes throw exposed explorers into the air. | Follow the green Shelter Beacon and get inside a **Calm Spot**, **Cottage doorway**, or **Cavern Rib Arch**. |
| **2** | **🌪️ Colossal Sneeze Gale** | A 5-second deep inhale suction pulls you backward, followed by a hurricane-force eastward gale and swirling vortex leaves. | Hide behind a **Boulder**, **Tree trunk**, **Cottage**, or inside a **Calm Spot**. |
| **3** | **🌑 Nightmare Eclipse** | Thick violet shadow-fog and dark pillars close in while 6 glowing **Nightmare Eyes** creep toward Pip. | Press **`H`** to unleash your golden **Hush Lantern Banishment Dome** and vaporize the wisps. |
| **4** | **🌠 Dream Meteor Shower** | Vertical skybeams and glowing target rings warn where blazing fire-tail meteors are about to slam down. | Dodge the impact rings, then collect fallen **Star Shards** for calm and a burst of speed. |
| **5** | **🌊 Sleep-Roll Gravity Tilt** | Old Mother Hush rolls sideways in her sleep, tilting the entire horizon (`±11.5°`) with glowing gravity waves and tumbling pebbles. | Counter-steer uphill or anchor yourself in a **Calm Spot**, **Vine**, or **Climbable Wall**. |

## ✨ THE DREAM FRAGMENTS

Scattered across the giant's enormous landscape are four mysterious **Dream Fragments**.

Each fragment is connected to a different region and contributes to the larger mystery surrounding the giant. As Pip discovers them — in any order — the world gradually changes and the giant's breathing cycle slows (`24s → 28s → 32s → 36s → 42s`).

The fragments eventually find their place at the Dream Altar, but discovering them is part of the larger exploration experience — not a reason to ignore everything else the world has to offer.

---

## 🐜 CREATURES OF THE GIANT

The giant's back is not an empty landscape. Small creatures have adapted to its unusual environment, creating lives and settlements across its enormous body:

- **Grandma Moss-Ant:** A warm, knowledgeable ant from the Tail Marshes who shares the secrets of the giant and the Hush Lantern.
- **Bramble the Beetle:** An inhabitant of the Spine Forest who understands the movement of its vines and wind.
- **Wisp-Moths:** Glowing creatures of the Rib Caverns whose movements reveal environmental patterns.
- **Old Pebble the Snail:** A quiet inhabitant of the Crown who knows stories about the giant and its forgotten past.
- **5 Roaming Explorer Ants:** *Scout Clover*, *Forager Pippin*, *Ranger Acorn*, *Delver Luma*, and *Stargazer Nim* wander autonomously across the marshes, valleys, forests, caverns, and ridges.

---

## 🧭 OPTIONAL MISSIONS & DISCOVERIES

The main storyline is only one part of Dreamback. Players are never forced down a single path:

- Turn off the optional golden firefly guide anytime with **`G`** for pure unguided free roam.
- Discover hidden corners, outer valleys, hamlets, and crystal grottos across the `780 × 340` world.
- Reveal hidden **Dreamfire Echoes** with your Hush Lantern (`H`) to uncover secret lore whispers.
- Chat with the 5 roaming Explorer Ants and regional creatures.
- Survive all 5 dynamic Living-Giant world events wherever you happen to be.

## 🌱 THE END OF THE JOURNEY

When all four Dream Fragments reach the altar, Pip finally completes the journey to the heart of the giant's dream.

The fragments rise from their sockets. The giant releases one long, peaceful exhale. Across the enormous landscape, 3,600 flowers begin to bloom. The fog slowly lifts, the sky changes into warm shades of pink and gold, and the world settles into a peaceful rhythm.

The journey ends not with the defeat of a creature, but with the restoration of a world.

> *«The Giant sleeps on. The colony is safe. Pip's lantern glows a little brighter.»*

## 🛠️ BUILT WITH

- **JavaScript (ES Modules)** — Core open-world, physics, breathing, and challenge systems.
- **Three.js 0.160.0** — 3D rendering, custom GPU grass/sky shaders, and post-processing.
- **CC0 Poly Haven PBR & glTF Assets + Procedural Canvas 2D Kit** — 39 PBR maps + 148 procedural storybook textures.
- **Web Audio API** — Breath-synchronized procedural ambience and sound effects.
- **HTML & CSS** — Minimal atmospheric HUD and overlays.

Dreamback is built as a browser-based 3D game, without a traditional game engine or a heavy build pipeline.

## 🌌 OUR IDEA

Most open-world games give players a landscape to explore.

Dreamback asks a different question: **What if the landscape itself were alive?**

Instead of treating the environment as a background, Dreamback makes it an active part of the gameplay. The terrain moves. The wind changes. The atmosphere responds. The world has its own rhythm, and the player must learn to live within it.

It is a small adventure across an enormous living world.

Because sometimes, the biggest world belongs to the smallest explorer.
